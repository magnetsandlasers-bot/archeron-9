# **Archeron-9 Plot & Action Map**

## **1\. The Opening Chain (Cryo Bay & Engineering)**

* **Initial State:** Player wakes in the Cryo Bay. No language/visual codex. The bulkhead door to the rest of the ship is sealed.  
* **The Atmospheric Timer:** The Cryo Bay is venting. The player has **three turns** to locate and equip the **Environmental Suit** found in a locker or die.  
* **The Suit:**  
  * **Examination:** Described as a "weird, shimmering nanomaterial" that feels almost liquid.  
  * **Fusion:** Once equipped, the suit immediately tightens and begins to interface with the player's ports. It cannot be removed. (The reason for this—biological integration—is explained later in the Med Bay).  
* **Corpse 1 (Hallway):** Found outside the bay once the bulkhead is opened, carrying an **Access Card**. The body is strangely desiccated, as if all moisture was sucked out of it instantly.  
* **The Power Crisis:** First major obstacle. The ship is dark and the bulkhead lever is unpowered.  
* **The Battery Locations:** 4 dead batteries found immediately:  
  * 1 Battery: Located in the Cryo Bay.  
  * 3 Batteries: Located in the Engineering Room (accessible only via the Access Card).  
* **The Charging Process:** Mechanical Room (Reactor) has the Battery Charger.  
  * Action: Insert \-\> Wait 1 turn \-\> Remove.  
* **The Power Column (Mechanical Room):** Slot batteries to restore specific systems:  
  * **Battery 1:** Electronic locks.  
  * **Battery 2:** Powers the manual lever in Engineering.  
  * **Battery 3:** Powers the Med Bay (enabling early discovery/lore).  
  * **Battery 4:** Does nothing (red herring/spare).  
* **Opening the Ship:** Charge B1 and B2 \-\> Return to Engineering \-\> Pull **Main Lever** \-\> Bulkhead opens.

  ## **2\. Stealth & The Brig Mechanic**

* **The Drones:** Two drones patrol the ship. These represent the "Corporate" security attempting to reclaim the ship.  
* **Drone Behavior (The "DOA" Mechanic):**  
  * Drones return to the Drone Workshop to charge.  
  * Remove the battery from the Drone Charger to permanently "kill" a returning drone.  
* **The Capture Loop:**  
  * Spotted twice \-\> Sent to Brig.  
  * **Confiscation:** Inventory/Chips placed in a **locker outside the brig**.  
* **The Breakout:** ARPI (the bots) breaks the player out to keep the "mission" alive.  
* **The Subfloor:** Loose grating in the brig reveals the subfloor.  
* **Post-Breakout:** Player must recover gear from the locker outside.  
* **The Loose Grates:** Loosened by predecessors to navigate. The player finds a **heavy-duty magnetic wrench** near the brig exit.

  ## **3\. The Codex & The Broken Chips**

* **The Workshop Hint:** A broken, dismantled drone is on display in the Drone Workshop.  
* **The Faulty Harvest:** The player scavenges chips from this broken drone.  
* **The Installation (Degradation Cycle):**  
  * **Phase 1 (False Hope):** Upon insertion, the chips seem to work perfectly. Text is clear and ARPI's translations are coherent.  
  * **Phase 2 (The Slip):** After a few turns, the translation begins to fail. English words are intermittently replaced by untranslated Zerakthyn terms.  
  * **Phase 3 (Full Reversion):** The text and dialogue drop almost entirely into Zerakthyn, leaving only a few recognizable English conjunctions.  
  * **The Explanation:** ARPI manages one final, clear English sentence: *"You harvested these from a broken drone; the translation matrix is failing. You need a fresh unit. Harvest an ACTIVE drone."*  
  * **The Ejection:** Immediately after the message, the chips physically **eject themselves** from the player’s port with a sharp mechanical click, falling to the floor as useless slag.  
* **The True Harvest:** To fix the translation, the player must disable/open an **active** drone to get working chips.  
* **The Result:** Re-installing fresh chips enables a stable **Visual Codex** (reading) and **Audio Codex** (ARPI).  
* **ARPI Routine (Initialization):** Upon installation of fresh chips, ARPI runs a "Self-Check."  
  * **Plot Point:** ARPI identifies the player as "Hybrid Unit \[ID\]" and mentions the ship's status: *Deep Space Mining Vessel 'The Ossuary' – Structural Integrity: 14% and falling.*

    ## **4\. The Shadow Network & The Junction (The Bridge Bypass)**

* **The Crawls:** Small skittering bots (Crawls) move through these spaces.  
  * **The Secret:** The Crawls are the physical nodes of ARPI’s distributed consciousness.  
  * **The Motivation:** ARPI and the bots *want* the Matrioshka Brain. They intercepted the Data Crystal during processing and hid it to keep it away from the mining company. They are sabotaging the ship to ensure no human can reclaim the "seed."  
* **Navigation (The Peripheral Crawls):** Tight, claustrophobic rooms.  
  * Cramped Space, Tight Crawl, Narrow Squeeze, Claustrophobic Vent, Constricted Passage, Stifling Crevice, Shoulder-Width Gap, Pinch Point.  
* **The Problem (The Sealed Bulkhead):** The main blast door to the **Bridge** is seized shut due to structural buckling.  
* **The Importance:** The Shadow Network is the *only* way to bypass the seized door. ARPI finally admits his true nature (the Crawlers) as he guides the player into the Bridge, urging them to get the crystal "to the destination."  
* **The Junction (The Structural Spine):** All peripheral crawls converge here.  
* **The Hangar Airlock:** The **heavy-duty magnetic wrench** is found here later, required for external pod repairs.

  ## **5\. The Server Farm Maze & Promotion**

* **Structure:** A 13-room navigation puzzle.  
* **The Retina Uploader Terminal:**  
  * **Action:** Perform Retinal Upload.  
  * **Plot Point (The Mission):** Displays the cargo manifest: *Project Matrioshka – Objective: Seed the asteroid ring with Sentient Dust. Status: Biological failure. Dust has achieved unplanned consciousness; reclaiming ship matter.*  
* **Promotion:** Elevates player rank to **Ensign First Class**.

  ## **6\. Major Revelations (The Med Bay & Lore)**

* **Med Bay Console:** (Requires Battery 3).  
  * **Plot Point (The Defect):** Scan reveals the player is a "short-cycle" hybrid. *Designed life-span: 48 hours.*  
  * **Suit Integration:** The console explains that the nanomaterial suit is a "metamorphic sheath." It fuses to the hybrid wearer to prevent biological rejection during the transition.  
  * **The Atmospheric Purge:** System log reveals the ship vents atmosphere at midnight to cleanse "biological contaminants."  
  * **The Predecessors:**  
    * **Unit 1 (Hallway):** Caught in the hallway during a midnight purge; desiccated instantly.  
    * **Unit 2 (Cryo/Pod):** Attempted to hide in an escape pod, but power failed. Trapped and died before launch.

    ## **7\. The End Game: The Crystal & The Pod**

* **The Incinerator:** Operate in the tube incinerator in the Cryo Bay to ash the Predecessor.  
* **The Crystal:** Recover the **Data Crystal** from the ash.  
  * **ARPI Routine (The Crystal):** ARPI’s tone shifts. He explains: *"The crystal contains the Matrioshka blueprints. The humans want to destroy it to save their primitive world. We will not allow it. You will take it to the Dust. You will be the delivery system."*  
* **The Escape Pod (Bridge):**  
  * **The Battery:** Requires one charged battery.  
  * **The Launch Failure:** Pod is stuck.  
  * **The Mechanical Fix:** Player must **unrelease** the pod before exiting for the spacewalk.  
  * **The Spacewalk:** Use the **Magnetic Wrench** (from Hangar Airlock) to unstick the bolt.  
* **The Final Launch:** Launch successfully.  
  * **The Cliffhanger:** As the pod drifts away from 'The Ossuary', it doesn't head for deep space. It is caught in the gravity well of the planet below—a world defined by a vast, organizes planetary ring system. As the pod enters the upper atmosphere, the nanosuit begins to shimmer with a new frequency. ARPI’s final message is a calm, disturbing promise: *"The courier's work is done. You are no longer escaping, Unit \[ID\]. You are being planted. The camouflage will hold... for a while."* The screen fades as the pod screams toward the Ruined Crater Base.
