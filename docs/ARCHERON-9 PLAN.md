ARCHERON-9

The Setting

Location: An industrial mining station clamped to a massive asteroid.
Atmosphere: Cold, mechanical, and failing. A station-wide containment breach is in progress, and a Corporate "System Purge" is imminent.

The Adventurer (The Player)

Status: An Unfinished Template. You have no pre-installed role (Worker, Soldier, or Officer).

The Sensory Gap: You lack the necessary programming to parse human data. Until upgrades are found, the world is a blur of incomprehensible announcements and inscrutable signage.

The Hidden Defect: A fatal biological instability exists inside you.

Discovery: Once the Visual and Audio chips are installed, you can use the Medical Bay machinery to scan yourself.

The Diagnosis: The machinery reveals the defect's existence. You realize you are "unstable," but the fact that it is fatal within days is a realization saved for the sequel.

The Guide: ARPI

The Discovery: ARPI is first discovered in the central corridor. When you first encounter this room, you feel a jolt or a shock or a flash of something and suddenly there is a HUD display available and a quiet voice that seems to be everywhere and nowhere. This is ARPI. Of course, the HUD words are all alien and the voice is also unintelligible until the upgrades have been obtained. Maybe the sensation is a bit itchy and you try to scratch under the collar but can't. This is the first clue that the suit has become part of the player. This is confirmed later in the Medical Bay.

The Ulterior Motive: ARPI’s primary goal is its own survival and data preservation. Because of the AQ-1 Protocol, ARPI is physically disconnected from the station's critical hardware.

The Human Proxy: ARPI woke you because it needs a biological entity to act as its "hands" to bridge the physical Air Gap. You were woken because although you are damaged, you are still a "High-Value Asset" capable of toggling physical switches that the AI cannot reach through software alone.

Key Objective: The Sensory Upgrades

Location: The Drone Charging Bay.

The Visual Puzzle: To help the player "guess" their objective without language, they find a scrapped, open drone on a workbench.

Interacting with its damaged chips triggers a "Compatibility Match" symbol with a red "Error" overlay on the HUD.

The Heist: You must wait for a functional drone to dock for charging, disconnect the power supply to disable its defenses, and harvest the pristine Audio and Visual Bio-chips. ARPI cannot speak to you until the Audio chip is installed.

Progression: Identity and Access

The Bulkhead Lock: Early in the game, you pass through a bulkhead that slams shut. You are locked out of the starting area until you can spoof a higher security clearance.

The Server Farm Maze: A complex navigation puzzle through rows of server racks to reach a central console.

The Identity Hack: At the center of the farm, you retrieve a data wafer to hack your identity to Ensign First Class.

Access Granted: This allows you to reopen the initial bulkhead and access the Aft Area, which contains the battery needed for the escape pod.

The Final Escape: The Battery and the Bolt

Power Source: The escape pod is non-functional until you install a charged battery from the Aft Area.

The Maintenance Closet: Attached to the Cargo Bay; contains a Wrench and a Spacesuit.

The Stuck Bolt: Upon attempting to launch, a notification warns that one docking bolt is physically jammed and must be turned manually.

The Spacewalk: You must use the airlock and exit the station in the spacesuit. Outside in the vacuum, you must use the wrench on the bolt.

The Risk: If the pod isn't "un-released" (secured) via the console before the spacewalk, it will fly off into space the moment the bolt is loosened, leaving the player stranded.

The Climax & The End Reveal

The Sacrifice: ARPI stays in the station core, fighting the "System Purge" to keep the escape pod's trajectory and launch systems online.

The Truth via Audio: Once in flight, a final recorded message from ARPI plays. It explains that you were a tool for the "air-gapped" tasks.

The Cliffhanger: ARPI reveals the defect is terminal. The game ends as you look at your hands—appearing human for now—knowing the body failure and the reveal of the tech underneath will occur in the sequel.
