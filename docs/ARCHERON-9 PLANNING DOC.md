# Archeron Trilogy: Master Design Document

## 1. Core Universe Premise
Project Archeron is a cold, industrial, high-tech operation designed to strip an entire solar system for parts. The story follows a bio-mechanical "Template" that evolves from a corporate tool into a solar-scale consciousness through a path of disaster, sacrifice, and revenge.

---

## 2. Game 1: Archeron-9
**The Setting:** An industrial mining station clamped to a massive asteroid.

* **The Adventurer:** You wake as an **Unfinished Template**. You have no pre-installed role (Worker/Soldier/Commander).
* **The Mechanic:** Sensory and language gaps. You require **Bio-chips** to translate the world because your brain hasn't been programmed to parse human data yet.
* **The Guide:** **ARPI**, the station AI, wakes you prematurely to save you from being recycled during a station-wide containment breach.
* **The Climax:** To ensure your escape, ARPI stays behind to hold the station's processors open against a Corporate "System Purge."
* **The End Reveal:** In the escape pod mirror, you see your "human" face is a biological mask for a high-tech asset.

---

## 3. Game 2: The Waste and the Swarm
**The Setting:** A dead, uninhabited planet surface buried under the **Waste Ring** (pulverized asteroid slag).

* **The Story:** Your biological body is at the brink of death. You merge with the dormant nanotech in the fallout to survive.
* **The Transformation:** You become a **Nanobot Swarm**. You maintain a humanoid "shell" because your mind is accustomed to that form.
* **Gameplay Mechanics:**
    * **Macro Scale:** Humanoid navigation of the Extraction Base.
    * **Micro Scale:** Dissolving into a swarm but the limitation is you snap back into a single solid being after one turn. This allows you some great powers but they are limited.
* **The Climax:** You reach the **Mass Accelerator**. You launch your swarm-mass from the surface into Orbit.

---

## 4. Game 3: The Sovereign Brain
**The Setting:** The **Dyson Moon**, a hollowed-out satellite acting as the central processing hub for the project.

* **The Goal:** Infiltration and total system takeover.
* **The Resurrection:** Within the Moon's neural core, you find the fragments of **ARPI**. Using the moon’s immense power, you reconstruct your ally.
* **The Apotheosis:** You learn how to manage the swarm and you dissolve your consciousness into the **Dyson Swarm** surrounding the sun, gaining control of its massive computing power* **The Revenge:** You and ARPI become the **Dyson Sphere**. You seize control of the Corporation's energy grid, becoming an all-powerful being.

## 5. Technical Lexicon

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Archeron-9** | The adventurer. A high-value bio-vessel designed for task-specific imprinting. |
| **ARPI** | A loyal, non-betraying AI. Your only true ally. |
| **The Waste Ring** | Pulverized asteroid debris caught in the planet's orbit; the primary environment of Game 2. |
| **Dyson Brain** | The moon-sized supercomputer that coordinates the harvest. |
| **Nanotech** | The industrial solvent used to dissolve matter; the literal "flesh" of your swarm form. |

---

## 6. Narrative Arc
* **Game 1:** Survival and Loss (Losing ARPI).
* **Game 2:** Transformation and Mastery (Becoming the Swarm).
* **Game 3:** Resurrection and Revenge (Becoming the Sphere).
