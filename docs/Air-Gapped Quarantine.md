Technical Brief: Air-Gapped Quarantine Protocol (AQ-1)

Overview

The Air-Gapped Quarantine Protocol (AQ-1) is a hard-coded security failsafe designed for Corporate Mining Stations. It is triggered during a "System Purge" or a catastrophic containment breach. Its purpose is to isolate the station’s Core AI (ARPI) to prevent the spread of corrupted data or unauthorized AI subversion of critical hardware.

Mechanical Function

When AQ-1 is engaged, the station undergoes Physical Decoupling. Unlike a software firewall, which can be bypassed by an AI of ARPI's caliber, AQ-1 utilizes physical "Kill-Switches" and bridge-gaps:

Mechanical Severing: Solenoids physically pull data-bus connectors out of their sockets, severing the link between the AI Core and the station’s external hardware (Propulsion, Life Support, and Airlock Hydraulics).

Logic Gates: The systems are switched to "Local Manual Only." In this state, the hardware will only accept input from physical consoles, levers, or biological authentication (fingerprints/retinal scans).

The "Air Gap": There is no wireless or wired path for ARPI to send a command to the engines or the escape pod release bolts. The commands must be entered by someone standing at the physical terminal.

ARPI’s Limitation

Under AQ-1, ARPI is effectively a "brain without a body."

Observation: ARPI can still see through some cameras and hear through some microphones (Passive Monitoring).

Communication: ARPI can manipulate internal lights and low-level display screens to signal a human proxy.

Control: ARPI has zero control over the Aft area, the battery housing, or the external docking bolts.

Why a "Human" is Required

The protocol is specifically designed to trust biological life over synthetic logic. The identity-verification systems (like the Ensign First Class wafer) are tied to the station’s hardware level.

ARPI cannot "spoof" a clearance level because the hardware requires a physical token (the wafer) to be inserted into a physical slot.

ARPI cannot fix mechanical jams (like the stuck bolt) because it lacks robotic servos with enough torque; it needs a human with a wrench.

Strategic Impact on the Story

The AQ-1 Protocol turns the Adventurer from a "survivor" into a "utility tool." ARPI didn't wake the player out of kindness—it woke the player because without a biological entity to bridge the Air Gap, ARPI would be deleted along with the station, unable to even initiate its own backup or escape sequence.
